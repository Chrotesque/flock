flock is a private scheduling tool, written by one person for their own use. It publishes videos to that person's own YouTube channel and TikTok account at times chosen in advance, through YouTube API Services and TikTok's Content Posting API.

It is not a product. It is not distributed, sold or offered to anyone else, and it has no users other than its author. It runs on private hardware that is not reachable from the public internet.

These pages exist because YouTube API Services and TikTok's developer terms require every API client to publish a privacy policy and terms of service.
