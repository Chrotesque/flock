flock is a private tool written by one person for their own use. It is not distributed, sold, licensed or made available to anyone else, and has no users other than its author.

These terms are published because YouTube API Services and TikTok's developer terms require every API client to publish them. They govern the only use that exists: the author's own.

What flock does

flock lets its author write text for a video, choose a release time, and publish that video to the author's own YouTube channel and TikTok account, using YouTube API Services and TikTok's Content Posting API. It also copies the video to a folder on the author's own storage.

Use of YouTube API Services

flock uses YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

flock uploads only content its author owns or is entitled to publish, to a channel its author controls. It is not used to access, collect or process information belonging to any other person or channel.

Use of TikTok's APIs

flock uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Videos are posted only on the author's explicit instruction, to an account the author controls, and only content the author owns or is entitled to publish. The author is responsible for what is posted, for disclosing commercial content where TikTok requires it, and for the music and other material in each video, in line with TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and Branded Content Policy.

flock uses nothing it receives from TikTok for any purpose other than posting, stores no TikTok content, and shows TikTok content nowhere.
